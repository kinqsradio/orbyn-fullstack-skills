What this prototype verifies

Real 3D part coordinates, camera turning, shared crank motion, fixed-length connecting rods, selectable cylinders and synchronized stroke readouts. Rendered with our maintained beta 3D → SVG path; no GPU is required.

Ideal rigid links and open bore guides. No combustion, valves, manufacturing clearances, sound, stress or production-engine accuracy is claimed.
