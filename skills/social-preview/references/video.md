# Produce a readable catch-up video

Use only for requested video output. Choose the actual review question, source
candidate, audience and final artifact. Reuse supplied real captures; label a
synthetic diagram/fixture. A storyboard, image set or FFmpeg command is not a video.
No recording of private windows/accounts or external speech call follows implicitly.

Build a short scene sequence with one decision/result per scene and a script tied
to actual evidence. A catch-up preserves unresolved human decisions and limitations
under [work recaps](../../orbyn-workflow/references/explanation-and-recaps.md).
Use an existing installed encoder and inspect codec/font/subtitle support before
execution; missing tools leave output blocked rather than silently installing.

Typeset subtitles within the chosen frame/safe area, with readable contrast and
line lengths. Choose reading holds from the actual text and audience; retain word
counts, chosen reading-rate assumption and transition allowance. Narration duration,
when supplied, also bounds the hold. Do not use arbitrary rapid slide timing or
claim a universal words-per-minute usability threshold.

Encode the actual file and inspect duration, resolution, frame order and subtitle
coverage with an available media inspector. Extract frames inside every scene's
hold and around relevant transitions; inspect their pixels separately from script
or container metadata. Play the video through an available player when claiming
playback/voice synchronization. Distinguish encoded/decoded frame checks from
human real-time playback. Keep editable scenes/script/subtitle timings, export
and evidence together under ignored task output. Preserve failed encodes.

Report what is encoded, what was visually inspected, hold assumptions and unavailable
playback/screen-reader/native/platform validation. A synthetic output does not
prove production video capture, representative-user comprehension or upload behavior.
