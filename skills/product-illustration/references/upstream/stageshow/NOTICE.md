Product names in the examples are invented. No real company's logo, app icon or
photograph is bundled, and no third-party fonts are bundled. Scenes use CSS and

`BrandMark` displays a logo the consumer supplies. That artwork stays under the
consumer's own terms.

`Phone frame="photo"` is optional. Supply your own permitted bezel image via
`--ss-phone-photo-light: url(...)` and optionally `--ss-phone-photo-dark`.
Without it, the kit uses its CSS-drawn `pro` frame. No bezel photographs are
bundled.

Phone, window and cursor geometry describes generic device conventions. It implies
no affiliation with, or endorsement by, any platform owner.
