# Third-party method notes

The bundled files describe how to apply a method's questions in this library's own words. They do not reproduce the original publications, decks, worksheets or catalogs, and implying endorsement by an owner is not allowed.

| Name | Owner and terms to respect |
| --- | --- |
| STRIDE | Microsoft; the per-element variant is described in the 2006 MSDN Magazine article by Hernan, Lambert, Ostwald and Shostack |
| LINDDUN | KU Leuven DistriNet; the GO card deck is CC BY 4.0. Do not paste deck text. If you quote supplied deck text, keep its credit, title, source and licence |
| Elevation of Privilege | Microsoft and Adam Shostack; deck material is CC BY 3.0 US |
| PASTA, OCTAVE Allegro, CORAS, Trike, hTMM, STPA | Their authors and institutions. Trike's methodology text carries its own permission notice |
| MITRE ATT&CK, CAPEC, ATLAS | MITRE Corporation trademarks and terms of use. Verify technique and pattern ids and the version against the official catalog; a guessed id is a defect |
| Cyber Kill Chain | Lockheed Martin trademark |
| CVSS | FIRST. Compute scores only with an official calculator |
| MAESTRO | Cloud Security Alliance article by Ken Huang; not a numbered standard or certification |
| OWASP MASVS, cheat sheets | OWASP; cite as sources of questions, not as a compliance assessment |
| NIST publications | NIST; cite by number, never as a compliance claim |

When a report names a method, record the source, version and date you used and what you could not access. A catalog lookup or a numeric score never replaces the analysis.
