# Collect and interpret user evidence

Use when a product decision depends on people's needs, current work or interaction
with a proposed flow. Desk/source research settles documented facts; it does not
supply real user observations. Work from existing authorized evidence first.

State the decision and research question, target users/task contexts and gaps in
existing material. Separate user, buyer, administrator and operational needs where
relevant. Choose interviews/contextual observation for current work, usability
sessions for a task flow, or appropriate quantitative analysis for prevalence and
outcomes. Do not replace an uncertain choice with a generic persona template.

For actual participation, obtain required consent and recruitment authority,
explain purpose/recording/data use, minimize sensitive collection and provide an
accessible way to take part or withdraw under the applicable policy. Include
relevant ability, device, experience and task contexts rather than only available
teammates. A convenience sample and a synthetic persona have explicit limits.
No recruiting, messages, recording or analytics collection follows automatically.

Use neutral, representative tasks and nonleading prompts. Observe what a person
does and where they need help; avoid coaching them into the solution or treating
praise and stated future intent as demonstrated adoption. A think-aloud test may
change behavior; capture that limitation. For AI flows, check expectations,
source/uncertainty interpretation, correction/manual continuation, denied actions
and recovery rather than asking only whether the answer looks convincing.

Separate raw observation, participant interpretation, researcher inference and
recommendation. Preserve contradictory findings and context; report method, sample,
selection, task conditions and uncertainty. Redact identifying quotes and do not
export private recordings into a shared skill or model prompt. Avoid claims of
population prevalence from a few sessions or causal benefit from an uncontrolled
comparison. Define metrics with denominators/windows and verify instrumentation
before interpreting an apparent change.

Return concise findings tied to the decision, supporting evidence and limits,
unresolved questions and the smallest next action that could change the decision.
Use [product discovery](../../orbyn-plan/references/product-discovery.md) for the
investment/experiment decision and [UI validation](../../orbyn-ui-design/SKILL.md)
for the actual prototype and observed interaction. A research plan is not completed
research; invented users, transcripts and results must never fill a missing sample.
