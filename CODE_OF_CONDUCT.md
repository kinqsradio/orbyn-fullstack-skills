# Community conduct

Keep discussion respectful, specific and focused on the work. Welcome newcomers,
credit others, and critique ideas without personal attacks. Harassment, threats,
discriminatory abuse, doxxing and publishing private information are not accepted.

The maintainer may edit/remove harmful content, close disruptive threads or limit
participation. For a conduct concern, request a private maintainer contact without
posting sensitive details publicly. Security vulnerabilities follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). This is a project policy, not a promise of continuous moderation.
